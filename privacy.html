Privacy Policy: Channel Tools

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Channel Tools ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to manage and monitor the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and is not used by anyone else.

What the app accesses

When the owner authorizes the app with a Google account, the app can access, through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, data about the YouTube channels the owner chooses during authorization: channel details, video details, public and private video statistics, and channel analytics (such as views, watch time and subscribers). The app can also upload videos, thumbnails and channel artwork to those channels.

How the data is used

The data is used only to display statistics to the owner and to publish and organize the owner's own content. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

Authorization tokens and any data retrieved are stored only on the owner's personal computer. The app does not send data to any server operated by the developer or by third parties, other than calls to Google's own APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any user data to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local token files also ends its access.
